## The best robot mowers for small yards, reviewed

### 1. Segway Navimow i105N / i110N: best overall for small, flat yards

**Why we picked it:** It's the lowest-cost credible RTK-plus-vision mower and Reviewed's value pick. The i105N covers 1/8 acre (5,445 sq ft) for about $669, and the i110N covers 1/4 acre (10,890 sq ft) for about $789. Network RTK with AI vision means no antenna and no boundary wire, the platform is widely reviewed, and at 58 dB it's quiet enough for early-morning or evening schedules. It carries an IP66 rating and includes GPS tracking with an alarm.

**Skip it if:** your yard has any meaningful slope or stays soggy. It's rated for 30% (16.7°) and owners report it struggles on slopes and in mud. The 7.1" deck is narrow, and cutting height is set by hand. If your turf is a full quarter acre, the i110N has no headroom.

## What's the minimum lawn size for a robot mower?

There's no technical minimum. A robot doesn't care if your lawn is smaller than its rating, but below a certain size the value case gets thin. Our view: under about 1,500 to 2,000 sq ft of turf, a cordless push mower costs a fraction of the price and takes 10 minutes a week. Above that, a robot starts to earn its keep, because it mows little and often, keeps the grass even, and gives you back every weekend of the season.

A robot can still make sense on a very small lawn if mowing is physically hard for you, if you travel a lot, or if the lawn is awkward to reach with a push mower (a terraced or fenced patch). The smallest tier among our picks is the Navimow i105N at 1/8 acre (the newer ANTHBOT M5 LiDAR is rated for a similar 5,382 sq ft), and there's no penalty for buying it for less grass.

## How to size a robot mower for a small lawn

Size by mowable turf, not lot size. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) with a house, driveway, patio and beds might have half that in actual grass. Measure each patch, add them up, then add 15–20% headroom.

| Mowable lawn | Square feet | Buy rated for at least | Picks that fit |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1/8 acre | 5,445 | ~6,300–6,500 sq ft | Navimow i206 AWD (0.15 ac), eufy E15, any 1/4-acre model |
| 1/5 acre | 8,712 | ~10,000–10,500 sq ft | Navimow i110N, i210 AWD, S4, GOAT O1000, A3 AWD 1000 |
| 1/4 acre | 10,890 | ~12,500–13,000 sq ft | eufy E18 (0.3 ac), LUBA mini 2 AWD (0.37 ac), Neomow X2 Air (0.37 ac), A3 AWD 2000 (0.5 ac) |
| 1/3 acre | 14,520 | ~16,700–17,400 sq ft | A3 AWD 2000; or see our [large-lawn picks](/posts/best-robot-mower-for-large-lawns/) |

Notice that several popular small-yard mowers are rated for exactly 1/4 acre. They're a great fit for a quarter-acre lot, but tight for a quarter acre of actual grass, especially with slopes. The LUBA mini 2 AWD's 0.37 acre (16,146 sq ft) sits just under the 1/3-acre headroom line, so it's fine for a simple third-acre lawn but tight for a hilly one.

## Narrow passages and side yards

Small lots tend to have the trickiest geometry: a front patch, a back patch and a narrow side yard linking them. Three things decide whether a robot copes:

- **Physical width.** Check the mower's body width on the spec sheet and leave a few inches each side. Compact models with 7–8" decks are small machines, but AWD bodies are bulkier than their decks suggest.
- **Signal in the gap.** A side yard between a house and a fence or a neighbor's wall blocks much of the sky, which is where satellite-based RTK loses accuracy. LiDAR and vision mowers (Sunseeker S4, GOAT O1000, Dreame A3 AWD, LUBA mini 2 AWD) handle these strips more reliably. Our [navigation explainer](/posts/robot-mower-navigation-explained/) covers why.
- **Gates, steps and surfaces.** Wire-free mowers can drive mapped paths between zones, including across pavers, but they can't open gates or climb real steps. The Navimow i2 AWD handles 1.5" steps; anything taller needs a ramp, or a separate zone where you carry the mower over.

If your yard is all corners and passages, our [best robot mower for complex yards](/posts/best-robot-mower-for-complex-yards/) goes deeper.

## Storage, docks and winter

Small yards are short on space, so plan where the dock lives before you buy:

- **Dock spot.** You need a flat area at the lawn's edge near an outdoor outlet, with clearance for the mower to drive straight in. RTK models also want open sky above the dock (and the ANTHBOT M9 needs its antenna nearby). Check the manual for the exact clearance. Our [setup guide](/posts/robot-mower-setup-guide/) walks through placement.
- **Weather.** Every pick except the ANTHBOT M9 (rating not confirmed) is rated IPX6 or IP66, so they can live outside in season. A simple mower garage keeps leaves and sun off the shell.
- **Winter.** Bring the mower indoors for the off-season and store it charged in a dry place. See our [maintenance guide](/posts/robot-mower-maintenance/) for winterizing.
- **Theft.** Front-yard mowers are visible. Favor 4G tracking (Navimow, eufy, Dreame, LUBA mini 2) over models with none listed (GOAT O1000). Our [theft protection guide](/posts/robot-mower-theft-protection/) has more.

## What to look for in a robot mower for a small lawn

- **Navigation that suits the lot.** Open, sunny lawns: network RTK plus vision (Navimow i-series) is proven and cheap. Shade, walls and fences: LiDAR (S4, GOAT O1000, A3 AWD, LUBA mini 2). Simple flat rectangle: vision-only (eufy) is the easiest setup.
- **Slope.** Measure it with our [slope checker](/tools/slope-checker/). Flat lawns can use anything. Above about 30% (16.7°), skip the i105N and eufy; above about 45% (24.2°), you need the A3 AWD or LUBA mini 2.
- **Edges.** On small lots, edges are a big share of the lawn. A built-in trimmer (GOAT O1000) or edge disc (LUBA mini 2, Dreame EdgeMaster) saves the most hand work.
- **Cutting height and adjustment.** Max heights range from 2.8" (ANTHBOT M9) to 4" (LUBA mini 2). Manual height adjustment on the Navimow i-series is fine if you set it once a season.
- **Running costs.** Some 4G plans need a subscription after the first year (about $33 a year on the Navimow i2 AWD). Factor that in, and see our [best budget robot lawn mower](/posts/best-budget-robot-lawn-mower/) picks if price is the main filter.
