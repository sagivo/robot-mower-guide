Robot mower replacement blades are small, double-edged razor blades that pivot on a spinning disc. They cost about $15–$50 a set and should be replaced roughly every 1–3 months of mowing: Husqvarna recommends every 2 months, and Mammotion says to flip its blades at about 50 hours and replace them at about 150. Buy genuine blades when your brand sells them on Amazon. Third-party packs are cheaper, but fit is the seller's claim, so check it.

This guide is compiled from manufacturer product and support pages and the Amazon listings in our accessory database (prices checked October 2026, and they change). We don't hands-on test blades.

## Why robot mowers use tiny pivoting razor blades

A gas mower uses one heavy bar blade that hits grass hard and rarely. A robot mower does the opposite: it cuts a little grass every day or two, so it only needs to snip the tips. That allows small, light blades, typically three on a disc.

The design has three advantages:

- **Safety.** The blades pivot on their screws, so they fold back when they hit a root, stone or toy instead of transferring the full impact. Husqvarna describes its Endurance blade as a "safety blade according to standards."
- **Efficiency.** Light blades need far less power, which helps battery life and runtime.
- **Cheap replacement.** Instead of sharpening a heavy blade, you swap inexpensive ones. Many are double-edged so you can flip them once.

The trade-off is that they dull faster than a gas mower blade, so blades are the main recurring cost of owning a robot. See our [robot mower maintenance guide](/posts/robot-mower-maintenance/) for the full yearly budget.

## How often to change robot mower blades

The honest answer is "it depends on your lawn," but manufacturers give useful anchors:

| Brand | Guidance | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Husqvarna | Replace every 2 months for "optimal mowing results and low energy usage," varying with grass type and yard size | Automower support center |
| Husqvarna | Endurance blades last "more than twice the lifetime" of its classic blades | Endurance blade product page |
| Mammotion | Flip blades at 50 hours, replace at 150 hours (tested on Zoysia grass) | Endurance Replacement Blades product page |

Hours translate to calendar time based on how much your mower runs. A mower working about 15–20 hours a week would reach Mammotion's 150-hour mark in roughly two months, which lines up with Husqvarna's advice. Owners and reviewers commonly report 1–3 months.

**Blades wear faster with:** sandy soil, twigs, acorns, pine cones, gravel edges, tough grass types and big lawns that run many hours a week. **They last longer on:** small, clean, frequently mowed lawns.

Husqvarna also recommends changing blades and screws as part of winter preparation, which means you start spring with a sharp set and nothing rusts in place.

## Signs your robot mower blades are dull

Don't wait for the calendar if you see these:

- **Torn, frayed or white-brown grass tips** a day after mowing. Sharp blades slice; dull ones tear.
- **Uneven patches** or tufts left standing.
- **A louder or rougher sound** from the deck.
- **Shorter runtime or more recharging.** Husqvarna links sharp blades to lower energy use.
- **Visible damage:** nicks, bends, rounded edges or a blade that doesn't pivot freely.

Ragged results can also come from grass packed under the deck, so clean the underside first. Our [troubleshooting guide](/posts/robot-mower-problems-troubleshooting/) covers other causes of a poor cut.

## Genuine vs third-party: are compatible blades OK?

Genuine blades cost more but fit by definition, and they're what the warranty assumes. Third-party packs (FourShow, KuddinX, EMBerg, Green Piece and others) often cost half as much per blade and come in bigger packs.

Be clear about what a third-party "compatible" claim means: **the seller says it fits**. The mower maker hasn't confirmed it, and neither have we. Before you fit them, compare one against your old blade:

- **Length and width** match
- **Thickness** is the same. A heavier blade can unbalance the disc.
- **Mounting hole** is the same size. Anthbot, for example, uses a slightly larger hole than some brands.
- **Screws** are included and match the originals' length and thread

Our table flags listings where the fit isn't fully confirmed, even for some genuine packs whose titles don't name every model. A sensible approach: use genuine blades first so you have a reference, then try a third-party pack if you want to save.

## How to change robot mower blades safely

Most swaps take 10–15 minutes. Check your manual first, since quick-detach systems like Dreame's work differently.

1. **Disable the mower.** Switch it off at the main power switch, not just the app, and enable any PIN or child lock so it can't start.
2. **Wear cut-resistant gloves.** The blades are small but razor sharp, and the disc can spin when you touch it.
3. **Lay it on a soft surface.** Turn it over onto a towel or the lawn. Don't rest its weight on a LiDAR dome or camera; support the body or use a stand.
4. **Clean the disc first.** Brush off packed grass so you can see the screws and check the disc for cracks.
5. **Remove each screw** with the tool supplied, usually a small screwdriver or hex key.
6. **Fit new blades with new screws.** Replace the whole set so the disc stays balanced. Husqvarna says all blades and screws should be replaced together.
7. **Threadlocker: only if your manual says so.** Many replacement screws come pre-coated. If you add any, use a small dab of medium-strength (blue) threadlocker on the threads only. Keep it off the smooth shoulder the blade pivots on, and never use high-strength (red).
8. **Check that every blade pivots freely.** Spin the disc slowly by hand. A blade that's stiff or clamped tight has lost its safety function.

Dispose of old blades wrapped in tape or in a small container so nobody gets cut emptying the trash. For pet and child precautions while the mower runs, see our [robot mower safety guide](/posts/robot-mower-safety-pets-kids-wildlife/).

## How to make blades last longer

- **Clear the lawn** of sticks, pine cones and toys before scheduled runs, or set no-go zones around problem areas.
- **Raise the cutting height** a notch in sandy or rocky spots.
- **Flip double-edged blades** once if your brand allows it, as Mammotion recommends at about 50 hours.
- **Keep a spare set on hand** so a nicked blade doesn't mean a week of ragged mowing.
- **Store the mower indoors in winter** with fresh blades fitted, per Husqvarna's advice, so nothing rusts.
