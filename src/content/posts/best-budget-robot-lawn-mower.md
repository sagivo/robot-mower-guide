## What "budget" buys you in 2026

Two years ago, a wire-free robot mower started around $2,000 and anything cheaper meant burying a boundary wire. That has changed. Every mower on this page is wire-free and uses real navigation: network RTK, LiDAR, cameras or a mix. If you're new to the technology, our [navigation explainer](/posts/robot-mower-navigation-explained/) covers how each type works.

[[chart:value]]

What you give up at this price is mostly size and muscle:

- **Coverage.** Nearly every budget model is rated for about a quarter acre or less. The Dreame A3 AWD 2000 (half an acre) and HOOKII Neomow X2 Air (0.37 acre) are the exceptions.
- **Deck width.** Budget decks mostly run 7 to 8.7 inches (the HOOKII X2 Air's 11 inches is an outlier), against 15 to 17 inches on flagships. That means longer mowing hours, though for a small lawn it rarely matters.
- **Drive and slopes.** Most cheap robots are two-wheel drive and rated for 30% to 45% grade. Only the Navimow i2 AWD and Dreame A3 AWD add all-wheel drive.
- **Extras.** Motorized height adjustment, edge trimming and multi-year 4G plans are rare under $1,200.

None of that matters on a flat, open quarter-acre lawn. It matters a lot on a steep, shaded one.

**Why we picked it:** The i105N (about $669, 0.125 acre) and i110N (about $789, 0.25 acre) pair network RTK with AI vision, the same proven combination Segway uses on its bigger models, at a price that used to buy a boundary-wire mower. ## The hidden costs of a cheap robot lawn mower

The sticker price isn't the full bill. Before you compare a $669 mower with a $1,099 one, add these up.

**4G subscriptions.** Mowing never needs a subscription, but 4G cellular (for app access away from Wi-Fi and GPS theft tracking) often does after a free period. Segway Navimow includes one year on models like the i2 AWD and i215, then charges about $33 a year. Some models offer no 4G at all (the GOAT O1000), and Mammotion's YUKA mini 2 sells it as a $129 add-on. Pricier flagships like the LUBA 3 AWD include three years.

**Blades.** Robot mowers use small pivoting razor blades that dull quickly and should be swapped regularly through the season. They're inexpensive, but check the price and availability of your model's blade kit before you buy, especially for newer brands. Our [maintenance guide](/posts/robot-mower-maintenance/) covers how often to change them.

**A garage or cover.** Most mowers are rated IPX5 to IPX6 and can live outdoors, but a garage keeps sun, leaves and hail off the dock and the mower. Some brands sell one; third-party covers are cheaper.

**Your time on the edges.** Except for the GOAT O1000, every budget mower leaves an uncut strip along fences and beds. Budget 10 to 20 minutes with a string trimmer every week or two.

**Installation extras.** The ANTHBOT M9 needs an RTK antenna with a clear view of the sky, which may mean a mounting pole or an extension cable. Network-RTK models also depend on cellular coverage at your address.

**Eventually, a battery.** Batteries wear out after several seasons. Our guide to [how long robot mowers last](/posts/how-long-do-robot-mowers-last/) covers what to expect.

To see how these costs compare with paying a lawn service, plug your numbers into the [robot mower cost calculator](/tools/cost-calculator/).

## When a budget mower is a false economy

A cheap robot is a great deal when it fits your yard and a waste of money when it doesn't. These are the common traps.

- **Your lawn has hills.** A 30%-rated mower on a 35% slope doesn't mow slower, it gets stuck. If any part of your yard is steep, the [Dreame A3 AWD](/mowers/dreame-a3-awd/) is the cheapest safe answer. Our [hills guide](/posts/best-robot-mower-for-hills/) goes deeper.
- **You have big trees.** Under dense canopy, RTK mowers lose their satellite fix and camera-only mowers struggle in shade. Pay for LiDAR (S4, A3 AWD, GOAT O1000, or the M5 LiDAR on a tiny lot) or read our [complex-yard picks](/posts/best-robot-mower-for-complex-yards/).
- **Your lawn is right at the rating.** Manufacturer coverage assumes ideal conditions. If your lawn is 10,000 sq ft, a 10,890 sq ft mower will be running at its limit with no slack for rain delays. Buy the next tier up or a model with headroom.
- **Theft is a real risk.** A mower without 4G tracking is easy to walk off with. See our [theft protection guide](/posts/robot-mower-theft-protection/).
- **You'll outgrow it.** Planning to reclaim a weedy back corner next year? Size for that lawn now.

If any of these applies, it's usually cheaper to spend $300 more today than to sell a mower at a loss next spring.

## How to buy a budget robot mower on sale

Robot mower prices are volatile, and patience pays.

- **Watch the seasonal sales.** Big discounts tend to cluster around the spring launch season, summer sales, Amazon's fall Prime event in October and Black Friday through Cyber Monday. In May 2026, deal sites reported Navimow's i2 AWD summer sale starting at $899. It's about $849 now.
- **Compare sale price with list price.** Dreame's A3 AWD lists at $1,999–$2,199 but has been selling for $1,099–$1,539. At list price it's not a budget mower, and the picks above assume sale pricing.
- **Buy last year's model when the new one lands.** Segway's X3 series dropped in price after the X4 arrived. The same pattern repeats each spring.
- **Compare tiers, not just models.** Sometimes a larger tier is discounted below a smaller one, so check every size before you buy.
- **Buy where returns are easy.** If you're unsure whether your yard suits a budget model, a generous return window is worth a few dollars.
